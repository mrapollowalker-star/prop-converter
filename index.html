Prop Studio Picture Converter

Picture won't load in Prop Studio? Three quick steps, free, no account or sign-in.

  1. Drop your picture below (or press Ctrl+V). It's fixed for VRChat right here in your browser.
  2. Press Save, then press Open Postimages and upload the saved picture there (no sign-in).
  3. On Postimages, copy the Direct link (it starts with https://i.postimg.cc/), paste it into Prop Studio and press Spawn.
Drop pictures or whole folders here, or press Ctrl+V to paste

One picture or a folder of hundreds: they're all converted, then you can save them all at once.

Only have a link? (Discord, a website, ChatGPT…) If it still opens in your browser: right-click the picture > Save image as, then drop the saved file above. Discord links stop working after about a day, so do this soon after it was posted.

Picture sheets

Each sheet holds up to 16 pictures. Save the sheets, upload them to Postimages (you can pick them all at once), copy each sheet's Direct link, paste it into Prop Studio and press Open sheet: tap any picture on it (or Spawn all).

Open Postimages

Good to know

  • On Postimages leave Expiration on No expiration, or the link stops working.
  • Links that work for everyone in VRChat start with https://i.postimg.cc/, https://i.imgur.com/ or https://i.ibb.co/ and end in .png or .jpg. Postimages' other links (the page link) won't work: use the Direct link.
  • Lots of pictures? Drop them all, press Make picture sheets (16 pictures per sheet), upload the sheets, and paste a sheet's link into Prop Studio, then press Open sheet.
  • Animated GIFs can't play in VRChat; this page keeps the first frame.
