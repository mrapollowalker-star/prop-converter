Prop Studio Picture Converter

Drop in any picture (WEBP, AVIF, GIF, BMP, huge PNGs, screenshots…). You get back a PNG or JPG that Prop Studio in Tavern of Tales can load. Nothing is uploaded; it all happens in your browser.

Drop pictures or whole folders here, or press Ctrl+V to paste

One picture or a folder of hundreds: they're all converted, then you can save them all at once.

2048 is the most Prop Studio takes. 1024 is lighter on Quest.
Auto picks PNG when the picture has see-through parts, otherwise the smaller JPG.
Makes the plain colour around the edges see-through, for a cut-out prop. Slide right to remove more.
The prop takes the picture's shape, so empty borders make it bigger than it looks.

Getting it into Prop Studio

  1. Convert your pictures above, then press Save (or Save all for a whole folder).
  2. Upload it to imgur.com/upload (no account needed). Everyone in the world can see Imgur pictures; other sites need “Allow Untrusted URLs”.
  3. On the Imgur page, right-click the picture and choose Copy image address. It should start with https://i.imgur.com/ and end in .png or .jpg.
  4. Paste that link into Prop Studio on your tablet and press Spawn.

Animated GIFs: VRChat can't play .gif files. This page keeps the first frame as a picture. To keep the animation, upload the GIF to Imgur and change .gif to .mp4 at the end of the link.
